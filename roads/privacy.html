Roads: Traffic Simulator Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what data the Roads: Traffic Simulator mobile game ("Game") collects and how it is used. Developer: Ferkan Terzioğlu. Contact: gallerytycoon@gmail.com

1. Account and sign-in

You can play as a guest or sign in with Apple, Google or Game Center via Google Firebase Authentication. We store an anonymous user ID and basic identity data provided by your chosen provider (e.g. name, email or Game Center player ID). We never see your password.

2. Player name and top 3

You choose a unique player name. Your player name and your best Endless score in each city are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and shown to other players in that city's Endless top 3. Your city progress is stored on your device.

3. Ads

The Game uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads. AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EU/EEA/UK consent is requested via Google's User Messaging Platform; on iOS tracking permission (ATT) is requested separately. If you decline, non-personalized ads are shown. See how Google uses data.

4. Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your achievements and best Endless score are sent to Apple Game Center.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is processed only by Google (Firebase, AdMob) and Apple (Game Center, Sign in with Apple) to provide the service.

6. Retention and deletion

Use Settings → Delete account in the Game to permanently delete your account, player name and scores, or request deletion by email.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

8. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Data controller: Ferkan Terzioğlu (contact: gallerytycoon@gmail.com). Personal data is processed to create your account and show rankings (performance of contract) and to show ads (consent / legitimate interest). Data may be transferred to servers outside your country through Google and Apple. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object and port your data; you can withdraw ad consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Send requests by email; you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated; the current version is published on this page.
